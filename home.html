About ABES

At ABES Engineering College, we ignite the flames of curiosity, knowledge, and innovation. Established over two decades ago under the Society for Educational Excellence, our institution has grown into a dynamic center of learning in Ghaziabad.

Alongside engineering, our academic programs include M.Tech, MCA, and MBA. Our values of integrity, excellence, and innovation guide a journey of transformation and opportunity for every student.
